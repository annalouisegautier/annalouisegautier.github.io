OPTIMA @ CHALMERS

optimizing multiple agents.

OPTIMA is the research lab led by Anna Gautier in the Data and AI Division of the Department of Computer Science at Chalmers University of Technology. The lab’s research centers around planning and decision making in multi-agent systems with complex, real-world objectives, with applications to multi-robot systems, intelligent transport, and AI-based agentic systems.
